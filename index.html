SenseScan privacy policy

Effective date: 18 October 2026.

Who we are

SenseScan is an accessible barcode and QR scanner app for Android by Martín Baldassarre. It is open source under the Apache License 2.0.

This policy covers the SenseScan app. Apps that embed the SenseScan library have their own privacy policies.

Summary

  • No account.
  • No ads.
  • No analytics or tracking by SenseScan.
  • No data is sold.
  • What you scan stays on your phone, except in two cases. If you turn on product lookup, a product's barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts and its sister databases, and a Spanish medicine's national code is sent to CIMA, run by the Spanish Agency of Medicines and Medical Devices (AEMPS).
  • Only if you confirm that you want to open a scanned link does SenseScan contact that site, to check whether it leads to a PDF document.

Camera and images

Camera frames, gallery images, shared images and clipboard images are processed on your device to read codes. SenseScan never stores or sends them.

The clipboard is read only when you tap the clipboard button.

Documents you choose or share with SenseScan (PDF, Word, PowerPoint, Excel and LibreOffice files) are read on your device to find codes in their pages and images. SenseScan never sends them. To read some of them it keeps a temporary copy in its private storage and deletes it when it finishes.

When you choose "Read the package text", SenseScan reads the text printed on the package with Google ML Kit on your device. The text and the camera images are never stored or sent. Only a name you decide to save is kept, as described below. A date read from the package is said but never saved.

What is stored on your phone

SenseScan stores your settings: language, theme, guidance channels, voice, torch, the product lookup switch, and whether hints were shown. There is no scan history.

SenseScan also saves the products it looked up, so they can be read again without internet and when product lookup is off. For each product that was found, it saves the name, brand and quantity, with the barcode number and the day of the lookup (the date only, never the time). For each Spanish medicine that was found, it saves the name, the laboratory, whether it needs a prescription and the web link and date of its leaflet, with its national code and the day of the lookup. Saved medicines follow the same rules as the saved products described below. Products and medicines that were not found are not saved. There is no count of scans and no list of when you scanned.

A saved product is looked up again after 30 days when you have internet. Without internet, or with product lookup off, an older saved product is still used. At most 500 products and medicines are kept in total; when there are more, the oldest are dropped.

Saved products are kept in a private file that is excluded from Android's backup, so they are never backed up or copied to a new phone. Android's automatic backup may still back up your settings to your Google account. You control that in Android settings.

SenseScan also saves the names you give to products it cannot find, with the barcode number: 1 to 80 characters, up to 1000 names, with no expiry. They are kept in a private file excluded from Android's backup, work with product lookup off and are never sent anywhere: a code with a saved name is not looked up online either. You can delete one name with "Delete name" on the result.

You can delete the saved products and names at any time with "Clear saved data" in SenseScan's settings. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes the settings, the saved products and the saved names.

Product lookup (optional, off by default)

When you turn product lookup on, the barcode number of product codes is sent over HTTPS to Open Food Facts to get the product name.

Before asking Open Food Facts, SenseScan checks the products saved on your phone. A product saved 30 days ago or less is read from the phone and nothing is sent. With product lookup off, SenseScan uses saved products only and never sends anything.

If the product is not food, Open Food Facts may redirect the request to one of its sister databases: Open Beauty Facts, Open Pet Food Facts or Open Products Facts. They belong to the same project and receive the same data.

Open Food Facts and its sister databases receive the number and, as with any internet request, your IP address. Read the Open Food Facts privacy policy.

Medicines are never looked up in Open Food Facts. If a Spanish medicine's code contains its national code, SenseScan sends only that six-digit national code over HTTPS to CIMA, the AEMPS public medicines database, to get the medicine's name and its leaflet. AEMPS receives the national code and your IP address. Read the AEMPS legal notice.

Google ML Kit

The code reader and the package text reader use Google's ML Kit on your device. ML Kit sends Google technical diagnostics and usage data over HTTPS:

  • device information: manufacturer, model and operating system version;
  • the app package name and version;
  • per-installation identifiers;
  • performance metrics and API settings;
  • because SenseScan uses automatic zoom: a scanning session identifier, zoom changes and the position of the detected code within the frame.

ML Kit does not send the images, the content of codes or the text it reads. Google states that it does not share this data with third parties.

More information: ML Kit data disclosure and Google Privacy Policy.

Actions you choose

Opening a link, Wi-Fi, contacts, calendar, the phone dialer, WhatsApp, and sending feedback by email hand the data to the app you choose. This happens only after you tap. The policies of those apps apply.

When you confirm that you want to open a scanned link, SenseScan first asks that site what kind of file is at the address, following its redirects. That site receives your IP address, as it would when a browser opens it. If it is a PDF document, SenseScan downloads it into the app's private storage and opens it with the PDF app you choose. Downloaded PDFs stay in the app's temporary storage: those older than one day are deleted when another one is downloaded, and Android may also delete them. If you choose "Save to Downloads", the PDF is copied to your Downloads folder.

If you email feedback, your message is used only to reply.

Children

SenseScan is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.
